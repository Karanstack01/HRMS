StackDrove Leave Policy

  • Casual Leave (CL): Max 3 contiguous days. Cannot be clubbed with Earned Leave.
  • Sick Leave (SL): Medical certificate required if requesting > 2 consecutive days. Exempt from sandwich rule.
  • Earned Leave (EL): Requires at least 5 business days advance application notice.
  • Sandwich Rule Active: If Casual or Earned Leave flanks weekend or company holidays on both sides, the intermediate non-working days will be counted as leave.
  • Approval Turnaround: Applications are routed automatically to your designated reporting manager.
For emergency leaves or policy questions, please contact hr@stackdrove.com.
